About Campus Crush

Made for students. Built for discovery.

Campus Crush is envisioned as a student-friendly educational website that brings useful official links and learning resources together in one accessible place.

This is the first version. You can personalize the profile card with your photo, update the name, and later add study notes, scholarship announcements, admission information, and your own educational articles.
